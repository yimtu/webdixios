## 1. Research question

The hero copy currently says:

> Convertimos complejidad institucional en sistemas que permiten entender, decidir, ejecutar y mejorar.

The conceptual problem is therefore not “how do we make a globe more impressive?”

It is:

> **What must happen to institutional complexity before an institution can act on it without pretending that the complexity disappeared?**

The frozen S00 rule adds a second requirement:

> **Mexico must become a perceptually important locus inside the global/systemic field, without turning the hero into a geographic claim or a conventional map.**

The concept must explain both requirements before visual research begins.

# 2. Evidence base

## 2.1 Complexity in public institutions is structural, not decorative

**Evidence type:** OFFICIAL / OECD  
**Source:** OECD, *Systems Approaches to Public Sector Challenges: Working with Change* (2017)  
**Checked:** 2026-09-05  
**URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/systems-approaches-to-public-sector-challenges_9789264279865-en.html

The OECD describes complexity as a core feature of contemporary policy problems and argues that traditional linear analytical/problem-solving tools are insufficient for many complex or “wicked” public-sector challenges. Systems approaches are valuable because they account for interconnections, changing structures and systemic effects.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

The hero must not communicate that Dixios makes complexity vanish. A credible promise is that Dixios makes important distinctions, relationships and dependencies **legible enough to act on**.

The correct conceptual movement is:

```text
COMPLEXITY
≠
SIMPLIFICATION

COMPLEXITY
→
OPERABILITY
```

## 2.2 Public-sector capability means steering, coordination, prioritisation and delivery

**Evidence type:** OFFICIAL / OECD  
**Source:** OECD, *Building Centre of Government Capabilities to Steer and Deliver Complex Priorities* (17 July 2026)  
**Checked:** 2026-09-05  
**URL:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/building-centre-of-government-capabilities-to-steer-and-deliver-complex-priorities_9899e2fe-en.html

The 2026 synthesis describes complex public priorities as cross-cutting and fast-moving, requiring coordination across policy areas, clear prioritisation and stronger delivery capacity. It specifically connects effective steering with alignment among institutional structures, planning processes and analytical capabilities.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

“Capability” cannot be treated as an abstract glow at the end of a data pipeline. In institutional terms, capability is the ability to convert understanding into coordinated choices and delivery.

A useful causal chain is:

```text
SEE RELATIONSHIPS
→
FORM A MODEL
→
PRIORITISE
→
COORDINATE
→
ACT
→
LEARN
```

The hero does not need to display every stage literally, but its central transformation must be compatible with this chain.

## 2.3 Systems thinking increases useful choice by exposing relationships and structures

**Evidence type:** OFFICIAL / OECD OPSI  
**Source:** OECD Observatory of Public Sector Innovation, *Systems Approaches*  
**Checked:** 2026-09-05  
**URL:** https://oecd-opsi.org/work-areas/systems-approaches/

OPSI frames systems thinking as a way to acknowledge interconnections and complexity, surface structures that hinder change and expand the set of choices available to governments. It also stresses that public-sector innovation capacity must be embedded in the operating system of government rather than applied as an isolated technique.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

The transformation should not be “many things become few things” merely for neatness.

It should be:

```text
UNRESOLVED RELATIONSHIPS
→
LEGIBLE RELATIONSHIPS
→
MORE INFORMED CHOICE
```

That distinction matters. Over-simplification can reduce capability; useful modeling preserves the distinctions required for action.

## 2.4 A regulator cannot control what it cannot adequately distinguish

**Evidence type:** FOUNDATIONAL / CYBERNETICS  
**Source:** W. Ross Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics* (1956); Law of Requisite Variety  
**Contemporary reference checked:** 2026-09-05  
**URL:** https://theashbyinstitute.org/theory/

Ashby’s Law of Requisite Variety formalises a structural limit: a regulator needs enough response variety to absorb the relevant variety of disturbances. Put plainly, a control system with too few distinctions/responses cannot reliably govern a more varied environment.

A simplified conceptual expression is:

```text
V(R) ≥ V(D)
```

where `R` is the regulator’s available variety and `D` is the variety of disturbances it must handle.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

The value proposition is not “we remove complexity until the institution sees a simple dashboard.”

A stronger systems interpretation is:

> Dixios helps an institution acquire the distinctions, models and response capacity needed to engage with the complexity it already faces.

This supports the phrase **capacity real** much more strongly than generic “data intelligence.”

---

## 2.5 Effective action requires an internal model of the relevant system

**Evidence type:** FOUNDATIONAL / PEER-REVIEWED CYBERNETICS  
**Source:** Roger C. Conant & W. Ross Ashby, *Every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system*, International Journal of Systems Science 1(2), 1970  
**Checked:** 2026-09-05  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/00207727008920220

Conant and Ashby show, under the theorem’s assumptions, that a maximally successful and simple regulator must embody a model of the system it regulates.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

A key transformation in the hero can be understood as:

```text
WORLD AS EXPERIENCED
→
WORLD AS MODELED
→
WORLD AS ACTIONABLE
```

The middle state is crucial.

Dixios should not be represented as bypassing complexity. It should be represented as **building a usable model of relevant complexity** so an institution can make better decisions and coordinate action.

---

## 2.6 Coordination has a scale problem, not only an information problem

**Evidence type:** PEER-REVIEWED COMPLEXITY SCIENCE  
**Source:** Yaneer Bar-Yam, *Multiscale variety in complex systems*, Complexity 9(4), 2004  
**Checked:** 2026-09-05  
**URL:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cplx.20014

Bar-Yam extends the requisite-variety idea by emphasizing the **scale** of responses and the coordination required among components. The paper discusses limitations of hierarchical central control when problems require responses across different scales.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

A global/systemic field and a local focal territory are not contradictory concepts. They can express two scales of the same problem:

```text
SYSTEM SCALE
↕
SITUATED SCALE
```

The hero can therefore make Mexico conceptually important as the **situated locus where a larger system becomes actionable**, without claiming that the country itself is the data set, client map or exclusive operating territory.

---

## 2.7 Complex public systems involve actors, institutions and processes across levels

**Evidence type:** OFFICIAL / UNDP  
**Source:** UNDP Programming Guide, “Identify and understand the problem”  
**Checked:** 2026-09-05  
**URL:** https://rolhr.undp.org/people-centred-justice-security/chapter-4-step-1-identify-and-understand-the-problem/

UNDP describes justice/security systems as combinations of multiple actors, institutions, laws, norms and processes interacting dynamically, with decisions and implementation shaped across local, regional and national levels.

**Conceptual implication for Dixios:**

“Institutional complexity” should be treated as heterogeneous:

```text
ACTORS
DATA
RULES
PROCESSES
DEPENDENCIES
TERRITORIES
DECISIONS
```

The current hero’s micro-language already names several of these categories. The concept should unify them as distinguishable parts of one operating environment rather than as technical decoration.

# 3. Conceptual synthesis

The research converges on one central idea:

> **Institutional complexity becomes operable when relevant relationships are observed, modeled and coordinated at the scale where action must occur.**

This gives Dixios a stronger conceptual role than “technology company with data.”

Dixios is positioned as a **capacity-building layer between a complex environment and institutional action**.

The transformation is not:

```text
CHAOS → ORDER
```

because that is too simplistic and suggests complexity is eliminated.

It is not:

```text
DATA → DASHBOARD
```

because that reduces institutional capability to information display.

It is not:

```text
WORLD → MEXICO
```

because that would imply geographic narrowing or a claim the project has not established.

The more accurate conceptual transformation is:

```text
UNRESOLVED VARIETY
→
MODELED RELATIONSHIPS
→
COORDINATED CAPABILITY
```

or, in the language closest to the brand:

```text
COMPLEXITY
→
OPERABILITY
```

with a critical principle:

> **Operability is not less reality. It is better organized capacity to engage with reality.**

# 4. Mandatory concept brief

## CORE IDEA

**Dixios turns institutional complexity into operable capacity by making the relevant relationships of a system legible, modelable and actionable.**

The system does not become simple. The institution becomes more capable of acting within it.

---

## VERB

### Primary verb: `RESOLVE`

Definition for this project:

> To resolve is to increase usable distinction: identify what matters, clarify relationships and produce a state that can support coordinated action.

It does **not** mean “make the problem disappear.”

Secondary verbs that can support the concept later:

```text
MODEL
DISTINGUISH
COORDINATE
FOCUS
ACTIVATE
```

But the visual research phase should treat **RESOLVE** as the primary transformation verb.

---

## INPUT STATE

```text
A high-variety institutional environment:

- actors
- information
- rules
- territories
- processes
- dependencies
- competing priorities
- changing conditions
- multiple scales
```

Characteristics:

```text
fragmented
interdependent
unevenly visible
moving
not reducible to one linear chain
```

The input is **not random chaos**. It already has structure; the problem is that the structure is difficult to perceive and operate.

---

## TRANSFORMATION

```text
OBSERVE
→
DISTINGUISH
→
MODEL
→
RELATE
→
PRIORITISE
→
COORDINATE
```

Conceptually, Dixios increases the institution’s ability to match the complexity of its operating environment.

The transformation should preserve enough complexity to support real decisions while reducing ambiguity about what relates to what, what matters now and what can be acted on.

Short form:

```text
UNRESOLVED COMPLEXITY
→
RESOLVED RELATIONSHIPS
```

---

## OUTPUT STATE

```text
OPERABLE CAPABILITY
```

Meaning:

- clearer situational understanding;
- distinguishable priorities;
- relationships that can be coordinated;
- decisions tied to a model of the environment;
- a repertoire of possible actions;
- feedback that can improve the next decision.

The desired final feeling is not “everything is neat.”

It is:

> **the institution can now move.**

---

## WHAT THE VISITOR SHOULD UNDERSTAND WITHOUT READING THE EXPLANATION

> **Dixios takes a world of many interacting signals and relationships, resolves what matters, and turns that understanding into organized capacity for action.**

If a visitor sees only “global technology company,” the concept has failed.

If a visitor sees only “data visualization,” the concept has failed.

If a visitor sees “complex system becoming readable and actionable,” the concept is working.

# 5. The role of Mexico in the concept

The hard S00 rule requires Mexico to be perceptually prominent.

Conceptually, Mexico should function as:

```text
SITUATED FOCUS
```

inside:

```text
A LARGER SYSTEMIC FIELD
```

This means:

- Mexico is not merely a location marker.
- Mexico is not necessarily the entire operating boundary of Dixios.
- Mexico is not a fake client/data coverage map.
- Mexico is not evidence of a headquarters claim.
- Mexico is the place where the abstract global/systemic field acquires a **specific scale of attention**.

The conceptual relationship is:

```text
GLOBAL VARIETY
        ↓
SITUATED ATTENTION
        ↓
OPERABLE CAPABILITY
```

This satisfies the user’s requirement without inventing institutional geography.

A future visual direction must make that focal transition obvious, but **this phase does not select how it is rendered**.

# 6. Conceptual hierarchy for the hero

The hero should communicate in this order:

```text
1. DIXIOS
2. A COMPLEX FIELD OF RELATIONSHIPS EXISTS
3. DIXIOS RESOLVES RELEVANT RELATIONSHIPS
4. MEXICO IS A STRONG SITUATED FOCUS WITHIN THAT FIELD
5. THE RESULT IS CAPACITY TO ACT
```

This hierarchy prevents several common errors:

- a beautiful globe with no transformation;
- a Mexico marker with no systemic meaning;
- decorative modules that do not participate in the concept;
- “AI/data” aesthetics that imply capability without showing the mechanism;
- a simplistic chaos-to-grid metaphor that destroys too much complexity.

---

# 7. Conceptual invariants

Future visual research and implementation must preserve these invariants.

## Invariant A — Complexity is not noise

The input may look unresolved, but it must imply meaningful heterogeneous structure.

## Invariant B — Resolution creates distinctions

The transformation should increase legibility, not merely reduce element count.

## Invariant C — A model mediates action

The conceptual middle state matters. Action should appear to emerge from a representation/understanding of relationships, not magic automation.

## Invariant D — Capability is active

The output should imply possibility of coordinated action, not just a cleaner picture.

## Invariant E — Mexico is focus, not claim

Mexico’s prominence is an art-direction hierarchy requirement. It must not fabricate coverage, clients, jurisdiction, headquarters or metrics.

## Invariant F — The system remains alive

The environment is changing. Operability is therefore adaptive rather than a one-time static sorting exercise.

# 8. Motion concept — meaning only, not implementation

S00 is required to investigate meaningful behavior.

At the conceptual level, motion should express:

```text
RESOLUTION
```

not generic activity.

## MOTION_PURPOSE

Show a transition from unresolved relationships to a state in which relevant relationships can be distinguished and coordinated.

## TRIGGER

Not selected in this phase. Visual/technical research must determine whether the concept is best expressed by entry, scroll, pointer, timed system behavior or a combination.

## ACTIVE_DURATION

Not selected in this phase.

## OFFSCREEN_BEHAVIOR

Conceptual requirement: no meaningful state should be lost merely because animation pauses.

## MOBILE_VARIANT

Conceptual requirement: mobile must preserve the same transformation even if choreography/density is reduced.

## REDUCED_MOTION_VARIANT

Conceptual requirement: show the resolved states with minimal transition rather than removing the conceptual transformation.

## ESTIMATED_RUNTIME_COST

Not evaluated in CONCEPT_RESEARCH. This belongs to TECH_RESEARCH.

### Motion invariant

> **Motion is successful only if a still frame before and a still frame after describe two meaningfully different information states.**

If movement only adds atmosphere, it does not satisfy the S00 motion concept.

# 9. Concepts explicitly rejected

## “Chaos becomes order”

Rejected as primary concept.

Reason: institutional complexity is not random chaos, and excessive simplification can destroy necessary distinctions.

## “Data becomes intelligence”

Rejected.

Reason: generic AI/analytics framing; says little about institutions, coordination or action.

## “Connected world”

Rejected.

Reason: describes the current globe metaphor but not Dixios’s transformation.

## “Mexico at the center of the world”

Rejected.

Reason: turns a hierarchy requirement into an unsupported geographic/ideological claim.

## “Digital twin of government”

Rejected.

Reason: over-specific technical metaphor and unsupported product claim; Conant/Ashby does not require a literal digital twin.

## “Control room”

Rejected as primary concept.

Reason: implies centralized command and dashboard aesthetics before the actual operating model is established.

## “AI operating system for institutions”

Rejected.

Reason: unsupported product positioning and generic contemporary AI language.

# 10. Concept tests for the next phase

Any visual direction explored in `03-VISUAL-RESEARCH.md` must answer **yes** to all of these before technical feasibility is considered:

```text
[ ] Does it show heterogeneous complexity rather than random noise?
[ ] Does it show an actual change in legibility/relationships?
[ ] Does it imply modeling or organized understanding before action?
[ ] Does its output imply capability/action rather than just cleanliness?
[ ] Can Mexico be a strong situated focus without becoming a literal coverage claim?
[ ] Can the transformation be understood without invented numbers or fake dashboards?
[ ] Can motion represent RESOLVE rather than merely animate presence?
[ ] Does it remain compatible with institutional restraint?
[ ] Is it conceptually more specific than “global tech / data / network”?
```

Any visual reference that fails these gates is aesthetically interesting but conceptually irrelevant to S00.
